The Last Chapter

A Story of Choices, Secrets and New Beginnings

Abdullahi Sheikh Aden (Karieson)

Dedication

To everyone who has reached the end of one chapter and wondered whether the story could begin again.

Author's Note

The Last Chapter is a story about endings, choices and the unexpected possibilities that can appear when one part of life comes to an end.

Sometimes we believe that an ending means everything is over. But life rarely follows the endings we imagine for ourselves.

This story explores the consequences of choices, the weight of hidden truths and the courage required to begin again.

It is a reminder that closing one chapter does not necessarily mean closing the entire book.

CHAPTER ONE

The End

The letter was waiting on the table when he returned home.

He knew immediately that something was wrong.

There was no name written on the envelope. Only his address, carefully written in handwriting he had not seen for years.

He stood beside the table without touching it.

For a moment, he considered walking away.

Some memories were better left buried.

But curiosity was stronger than fear.

He opened the envelope.

There was only one sentence inside.

"You thought the story ended ten years ago."

He read the sentence again.

Then he looked toward the window.

Outside, the evening was beginning to fall.

For the first time in ten years, he understood that the past had not forgotten him.
