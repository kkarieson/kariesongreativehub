The Missing Letter

A Story of Secrets, Mystery and the Search for Truth

Abdullahi Sheikh Aden (Karieson)

Dedication

To everyone who has ever searched for an answer, followed a forgotten clue, or discovered that the truth can remain hidden for much longer than anyone expects.

Author's Note

The Missing Letter is a work of mystery and suspense fiction.

The story explores unanswered questions, forgotten memories and the secrets that people sometimes carry for years.

A single piece of information can change the way we understand the past. Sometimes, however, finding that information creates even more questions.

This story follows the discovery of a mysterious letter and the chain of events that begins when its contents are finally revealed.

The characters, places and events in this story are fictional and are presented for storytelling purposes.

Chapter One

The Envelope

The envelope had no return address.

Sarah found it beneath the front door when she arrived home that evening.

Her name was written across the front in black ink.

She stared at it for several seconds before picking it up.

There was something familiar about the handwriting.

She walked inside and placed the envelope on the table.

For some reason, she did not want to open it.

Finally, curiosity defeated hesitation.

She carefully tore the envelope open.

Inside was a single sheet of paper.

There were only three lines.

“You were told the wrong story.”

Beneath those words was a date from fifteen years earlier.

Sarah's hands became still.

She knew that date.

It was the day her father had disappeared.

For the first time in fifteen years, Sarah wondered whether she had ever really known what happened.
