Voices of Tomorrow

A Story of Young Voices, Social Change and New Dreams

Abdullahi Sheikh Aden (Karieson)

Dedication

To the young people who continue to dream, question, create and speak about the kind of future they hope to build.

Author's Note

Voices of Tomorrow is a contemporary story exploring young voices, social change and the dreams of a new generation.

Every generation grows up in a world shaped by the decisions of those who came before it, while carrying the responsibility of imagining what comes next.

Young people often experience change first. They see new possibilities, question old expectations and search for opportunities to create something better.

This story explores friendship, ambition, social expectations and the courage to speak when remaining silent feels easier.

The voices of tomorrow begin today, in the choices, questions and dreams of ordinary people.

CHAPTER ONE

A Different Voice

The room became quiet when she stood up.

Everyone had been talking for almost an hour, but no one had offered a solution.

She looked around the small hall.

Some people were sitting with their arms crossed. Others were looking at their phones.

She took a deep breath.

"Maybe we should stop waiting for someone else to change things."

A few people looked up.

She continued.

"We keep saying that young people are the future. But we are already part of the present."

Nobody interrupted her.

For the first time that evening, everyone seemed to be listening.

Her friend smiled from the back of the room.

They had talked about ideas like this many times. They had complained about the problems around them, imagined different possibilities and wondered whether ordinary people could actually make a difference.

But talking had always been easier than acting.

Tonight felt different.

"So what do we do?" someone finally asked.

She looked at the people around her.

She did not have all the answers.

But perhaps they did not need to have them yet.

"We begin," she said.

And for the first time, the room filled with ideas instead of complaints.
