Dreams Don't Wait

An Inspiring Story of Dreams, Courage and Perseverance

Abdullahi Sheikh Aden (Karieson)

Dedication

To every young dreamer who has faced doubt, obstacles and setbacks, yet continues to believe that a better future is possible.

Author's Note

Dreams Don't Wait is an inspiring young adult story about pursuing dreams despite doubt, obstacles and unexpected setbacks.

Dreams can begin as simple ideas, but turning them into reality requires patience, courage and determination.

Along the way, there will always be people who doubt us, circumstances that slow us down and moments when giving up seems easier than continuing.

This story explores ambition, friendship, perseverance and the importance of believing in a possibility even when the road ahead is uncertain.

A dream does not promise an easy journey. It simply gives us a reason to keep walking.

CHAPTER ONE

The Dream

The notebook had been under his bed for three years.

Inside were pages filled with ideas, drawings, plans and sentences describing the life he hoped to build one day.

Nobody knew how important that notebook was to him.

His friends laughed whenever he talked about his dream.

"Maybe one day," they would say.

Sometimes he laughed with them.

But secretly, he never stopped believing.

One evening, he opened the notebook again.

The first page contained a sentence he had written years earlier.

I will build something that matters.

He read the words slowly.

He had changed since writing them.

He had become older. He had experienced failure. He had watched opportunities disappear. He had listened to people tell him to choose something safer.

But the dream was still there.

He closed the notebook and looked at the clock.

Tomorrow, he would take the first real step.

He was afraid.

But fear was no longer going to make the decision for him.

The next morning, he woke before sunrise.

He picked up his bag and walked toward the door.

The dream had waited long enough.

Now it was his turn to move.
