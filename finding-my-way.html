Finding My Way

A Coming-of-Age Story of Friendship, Identity and Dreams

Abdullahi Sheikh Aden (Karieson)

Dedication

To everyone who is still discovering who they are, where they belong, and what kind of future they want to create.

Author's Note

Finding My Way is a coming-of-age story about friendship, identity, dreams and discovering one's place in the world.

Growing up is not always a straight path. There are moments when expectations from family, friends and society can make it difficult to understand what we truly want.

This story explores the uncertainty that comes with becoming an adult, the importance of friendship and the courage required to make choices for oneself.

Along the way, we discover that finding our direction does not always mean having all the answers.

Sometimes finding your way begins with simply being honest about where you are.

CHAPTER ONE

The Question

"So, what do you want to do with your life?"

The question sounded simple.

But he had never known how to answer it.

He sat quietly at the edge of the football field, watching his friends talk and laugh nearby.

Everyone seemed to have a plan.

One wanted to become a doctor. Another dreamed of starting a business. Someone else was already talking about moving to another city.

He looked down at his hands.

What did he want?

For years, he had followed the path others had suggested for him.

Study hard. Get good results. Find a stable career. Build a successful life.

It sounded like a good plan.

Yet something inside him remained uncertain.

He wanted to discover what made him happy. He wanted to understand his strengths. He wanted to know whether the dreams he carried were truly his own.

His friend sat beside him.

"You don't have to know everything today," she said.

He smiled.

Maybe she was right.

Perhaps growing up was not about knowing exactly where you were going.

Perhaps it was about having the courage to take the next step and discover the road along the way.

He stood up.

For the first time, the uncertainty did not feel quite so frightening.
