Dedication

To everyone who has ever carried a memory of someone they loved, and to those who discovered that some connections remain in the heart long after life has taken people in different directions.

Author's Note

When the Hearts Remember is a story about the memories that remain with us long after moments have passed.

Sometimes life takes people away from places, friendships and relationships that once meant everything. Time moves forward, circumstances change, and people grow. Yet there are memories that refuse to disappear.

This story explores the complicated relationship between the past and the present, and the way old feelings can return when we least expect them.

It is also a story about forgiveness, understanding and the courage to face the emotions we have tried to leave behind.

Because sometimes the heart remembers what the mind has spent years trying to forget.

Chapter One

The Memory That Returned

She had forgotten the sound of his voice.

At least, that was what she had told herself for years.

Life had become busy enough to make the past feel distant. There were new responsibilities, new places and new people. The memories that once occupied her thoughts every evening had slowly become quieter.

She had learned not to look back.

Until that afternoon.

She was walking through the crowded street when she heard a voice behind her.

It was only a few words.

But her heart recognized them before her mind could understand why.

She stopped.

For a moment, everything around her seemed to disappear. The sound of vehicles, the conversations of strangers and the movement of people around her all became distant.

Slowly, she turned around.

And there he was.

The person she had spent years trying not to remember.

He looked older now. There were changes in his face, changes that only time could create. But his eyes were the same.

Their eyes met.

Neither of them spoke.

Yet in that single moment, years of silence suddenly felt like yesterday.
