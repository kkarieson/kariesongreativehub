Before the New Dawn

A Story of Courage, Survival and Hope

Abdullahi Sheikh Aden (Karieson)

Dedication

To those who endured difficult times with courage, to those who carried hope through uncertainty, and to every generation that believed tomorrow could be different.

Author's Note

Before the New Dawn is a work of historical fiction.

The story explores courage, survival, family and hope during a period of profound social change.

Historical change is not experienced only through dates and major events. It is also experienced in homes, communities and families whose lives are changed by the world around them.

This story focuses on ordinary people facing extraordinary circumstances and the choices they must make to protect their families and preserve hope.

The characters and events are fictional and are presented for storytelling purposes.

Chapter One

Before Sunrise

The village was still asleep when Mariam opened the door.

The darkness outside was slowly beginning to change, although the first light of morning had not yet appeared.

She stood quietly and listened.

For several days, the village had lived with uncertainty. Conversations had become quieter, doors were closed earlier and people no longer gathered outside in the evenings as they once had.

Mariam looked toward the hills.

Somewhere beyond them was the road her brother had taken three days earlier.

He had promised to return.

She wanted to believe him.

Behind her, her mother called her name.

“Mariam.”

She turned.

Her mother was standing in the doorway.

“Come inside. It is almost morning.”

Mariam looked toward the horizon one more time.

A thin line of light had appeared beyond the hills.

The night was ending.

Neither of them knew what the new day would bring.

But for the first time in many days, Mariam allowed herself to hope.
