The Next Step

A Story of Friendship, Choices and Growing Up

Abdullahi Sheikh Aden (Karieson)

Dedication

To everyone standing at a crossroads, uncertain about what comes next but brave enough to keep moving forward.

Author's Note

The Next Step is a young adult story about difficult choices, friendship and the uncertainty that comes with growing up.

Growing up means making decisions without always knowing where those decisions will lead.

Sometimes the people around us expect us to follow one path while our own dreams point somewhere else.

This story explores friendship, responsibility, uncertainty and the importance of learning from the choices we make.

We may not always know what lies ahead, but there is always another step waiting to be taken.

CHAPTER ONE

The Decision

The letter was still sitting on the table.

He had read it three times, but the words had not become any easier to understand.

He had been accepted.

It was the opportunity he had been waiting for.

There was only one problem.

Accepting it meant leaving home.

He looked through the window at the familiar street.

This was where he had grown up.

Every corner carried a memory.

The shop where he and his friends used to buy snacks after school. The field where they had spent countless afternoons. The old tree where they had once promised each other that they would never lose contact.

Now everything felt different.

His phone rang.

It was his best friend.

"Have you decided?"

He looked at the letter again.

"Not yet."

There was silence on the other end.

Then his friend said something he had not expected.

"Maybe you don't have to know whether it's the perfect choice. Maybe you just have to decide whether it's the next step."

He smiled.

Perhaps that was all he needed.

He picked up the pen.

Then he signed the letter.

The decision had been made.

Now the real journey could begin.
