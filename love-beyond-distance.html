Love Beyond the Distance

A Story of Love, Distance and the Heart's Journey

Abdullahi Sheikh Aden (Karieson)

Dedication

To every heart that has loved across distance, waited through uncertainty, and continued to believe that genuine love can survive the miles between two people.

Author's Note

Love Beyond the Distance is a story about the spaces that can exist between people who deeply care for one another.

Distance is not always measured in miles. Sometimes it is created by circumstances, silence, fear, family expectations, or the choices we make when life becomes difficult.

This story explores love not as something that is always easy, but as something that requires patience, honesty, sacrifice and courage.

Through the journey of its characters, we are reminded that loving someone can sometimes mean learning how to wait, how to trust, and how to understand what truly matters.

CHAPTER ONE

The Distance Between Us

The first thing she noticed that morning was the silence.

It was not the peaceful kind of silence that came before sunrise. It was the kind that made a person look at the phone again and again, hoping that the next time the screen lit up, everything would somehow feel different.

She sat quietly beside the window, watching the early morning light spread across the room. Outside, the world was beginning another ordinary day. People were walking to work, shops were opening, and somewhere in the distance a vehicle moved slowly along the road.

But for her, the morning had stopped being ordinary a long time ago.

She picked up her phone once more.

Nothing.

She smiled sadly and placed it back on the table.

Distance had a strange way of changing time. A minute could feel like an hour when you were waiting for someone you loved. A day could feel like a month when there were questions that remained unanswered.

She had once believed that love made everything easier.

Now she understood that sometimes love simply gave you a reason to keep going when everything was difficult.
