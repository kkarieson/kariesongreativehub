Between Us

A Story of Relationships, Misunderstandings and Understanding

Abdullahi Sheikh Aden (Karieson)

Dedication

To everyone who has ever struggled to say what they truly feel, and to those who have discovered that honest conversations can change the distance between two people.

Author's Note

Between Us is a story about relationships, misunderstandings and the conversations that can change everything.

Relationships are often shaped by the things we say, but also by the things we leave unsaid.

A misunderstanding can begin with a small moment and grow into a distance that neither person knows how to cross.

This story explores communication, trust, relationships and the importance of listening before making assumptions.

Sometimes what stands between two people is not a lack of love, but a conversation that never happened.

CHAPTER ONE

What Was Left Unsaid

They had not spoken properly for three days.

It was strange because they had once talked about everything.

Small things. Serious things. Dreams they were afraid to tell anyone else.

Now every conversation seemed to end before it had really begun.

She sat on the edge of the bed and looked at her phone.

There was a message waiting.

Are you okay?

She read it several times.

She wanted to answer honestly.

No, she was not okay.

She was hurt.

But she was also angry, and she did not know which feeling had become stronger.

Instead of replying, she placed the phone beside her.

Across town, he was doing exactly the same thing.

He had typed a message several times and deleted it each time.

He wanted to explain what had happened.

He wanted her to understand that things were not as they appeared.

But every possible explanation sounded like an excuse.

He looked at the unfinished message.

Maybe silence was easier.

But somewhere inside, he knew that silence was also creating a story of its own.

The next evening, they unexpectedly met.

Neither of them had planned it.

They stood facing each other, both waiting for the other person to speak.

Finally, she broke the silence.

"Why didn't you tell me?"

He looked at her.

There were many answers he could have given.

Instead, he said the truth.

"I was afraid."

She was quiet.

For the first time in three days, neither of them tried to walk away.

Perhaps the distance between them had never been as large as they had imagined.

Perhaps they simply needed to begin talking again.
