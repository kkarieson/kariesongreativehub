Across the Unknown

A Story of Exploration, Discovery and Courage

Abdullahi Sheikh Aden (Karieson)

Dedication

To the explorers who step beyond the familiar, to those who follow roads that have never been travelled, and to everyone who discovers that uncertainty can become the beginning of an extraordinary journey.

Author's Note

Across the Unknown is a work of adventure fiction.

The story follows characters who choose to leave the familiar behind and venture into places they have never seen.

Exploration requires more than curiosity. It requires preparation, courage and the willingness to continue when the path ahead becomes uncertain.

Through discovery and unexpected challenges, the characters learn that the greatest part of an adventure is sometimes not the destination, but everything discovered along the way.

The characters, places and events in this story are fictional and are presented for storytelling purposes.

Chapter One

Beyond the Map

The last mark on the map was a small black line.

After that, there was nothing.

Amira held the map against the morning wind and studied it carefully.

The route had been drawn decades earlier by an explorer who had travelled through the region and recorded what he had seen.

But beyond the mountain range, the map became empty.

“Are you sure about this?” Hassan asked.

Amira folded the map.

“No.”

Hassan looked at her in surprise.

She smiled.

“That's why we're going.”

They packed their supplies and began walking toward the mountains.

With every step, the familiar world disappeared farther behind them.

Ahead was a place that existed only as a blank space on a map.

And they were about to discover what was there.
